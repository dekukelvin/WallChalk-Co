INTRO.

WallChalk & Co. is a creative agency founded by David Adjei Coffie, a Ghanaian creative and innovative individual. At its foundation, WallChalk & Co. exists to assist individuals, businesses, institutions, artists and personal brands express who they are through thoughtful and original creative work. We listen, adapt and reflect your identity.
